# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a hockey fan project, not a place worth being unpleasant
over.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about a roster projection.
- Keep discussion on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, and any other
communication about the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening an issue or contacting the repository maintainers.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not follow this
code, and may block repeat offenders.
